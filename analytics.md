  to `PURCHASED` (helps quantify impulse control).
- `Desire Rating vs. Purchase Conversion`: A chart showing which desire ratings (1–10) actually
  result
  in purchases versus items that sit untouched or get deleted.
- `Wishlist Pipeline Value`: Total monetary value required to clear all current items marked as `READY` to buy.
- `Category Breakdown` (Donut/Pie Chart): Visual spending distribution showing where most of your
  money or item count goes (e.g., Tech vs. Personal vs. Home).
- `Monthly Spending Trend` (Bar Chart): Total amount spent on items month-over-month.
- `End-of-Life Distribution` (Pie Chart): Percentage split of why items leave active state (`USED_UP`
  vs `BROKEN` vs `RETIRED` vs `LOST`).
- `Average Cool-Off Duration`: Average number of days an item spends in `READY` state before moving to `PURCHASED`.
- `Wishlist Conversion Rate`: Percentage of wishlisted items that actually get bought versus items that end up canceled or permanently stalled.
